## BE-FAST
- **B**alance loss
- **E**yes (vision loss)
- **F**ace drooping
- **A**rm weakness
- **S**peech difficulty
- **T**ime to call emergency services

Any sudden BE-FAST sign is an emergency.

## Actions
1. Call emergency services immediately and record the time the patient was last known to be well. Eligibility for clot-dissolving treatment depends on that time.
2. Do not give food, drink or medication by mouth.
3. For patients after a stroke admission, a new drop in consciousness score (GCS) or new confusion requires immediate physician review.
