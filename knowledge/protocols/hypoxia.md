id: P-HYPOXIA
title: Low oxygen saturation detected by a wearable (demo protocol)
---
## Verify the measurement
Motion, poor perfusion (cold hands), a loose strap, nail varnish and tattoos can all cause falsely low SpO2 readings on wrist devices. Check the signal-quality value and request a repeat spot measurement with the patient seated and still.

## Thresholds (patients without a personalised target)
- Below 92 %: review within 30 minutes and ask about breathlessness.
- Below 90 %: urgent clinician review.
- Below 85 % on a good-quality reading: emergency response.

## Actions
1. Request a repeat spot check and a symptom check-in at once; do not wait for the next scheduled measurement.
2. If the low reading is confirmed, or symptoms are present, escalate according to the thresholds above.
3. For patients with COPD or another prescribed target, apply their personal thresholds (see P-COPD).
