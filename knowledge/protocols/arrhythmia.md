title: Fast irregular heart rhythm, e.g. AF with rapid ventricular response (demo protocol)
---
## Recognition
A sustained heart rate above 110-130/min with high beat-to-beat variability suggests atrial fibrillation with a rapid ventricular response or another tachyarrhythmia. A wrist PPG sensor cannot diagnose the rhythm; an ECG is required.

## Danger signs
Chest pain, fainting or near-fainting, breathlessness at rest, a systolic blood pressure below 90 mmHg, or new confusion. Any danger sign means immediate escalation to emergency services.

## Actions
1. Without danger signs, the RPM nurse contacts the patient, checks symptoms and medication (rate control, anticoagulation), and arranges an ECG and physician review the same day.
2. In an ICU context, notify the bedside physician immediately and obtain a 12-lead ECG.
3. Remember that new AF raises stroke risk; check for neurological symptoms (see P-STROKE).
