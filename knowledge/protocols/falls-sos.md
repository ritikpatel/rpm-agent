id: P-FALLS
title: Fall detection and SOS activation (demo protocol)
---
## Immediate response
An SOS press or a detected fall is always treated as critical until proven otherwise. The RPM team attempts voice contact at once.

## If the patient does not respond
Dispatch emergency services to the registered address and notify the emergency contact. Do not wait for a second attempt.

## If the patient responds
Ask about head injury, loss of consciousness, new pain, inability to get up, and whether they take anticoagulants. Head injury on anticoagulants, loss of consciousness, or inability to get up all require emergency assessment.

## After the event
Record the event and review medications that raise fall risk (sedatives, antihypertensives). A fall in a recently discharged older adult is a strong predictor of readmission.
