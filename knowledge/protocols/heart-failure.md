title: Heart failure decompensation monitoring (demo protocol)
---
## Early warning signs
- weight gain of more than 2 kg in 3 days
- increasing breathlessness, especially when lying flat or at night
- new or worsening ankle swelling
- resting heart rate persistently above the patient's baseline
- falling SpO2 or a rising respiratory rate

## Wearable patterns
A sustained rise in resting heart rate over several hours, with reduced step counts and lower heart-rate variability, often precedes symptoms by one or more days. Treat this combination as a trigger for a check-in call, not for immediate emergency action.

## Actions
1. For early signs, the RPM nurse calls the patient the same day, reviews weight, symptoms and medication adherence, and informs the heart-failure physician. Any diuretic change requires a physician order.
2. For SpO2 below 90 %, breathlessness at rest, chest pain, or confusion, escalate to the on-call physician immediately and consider emergency transfer.
3. Increase monitoring frequency until the trend reverses.

## Readmission context
Heart failure has one of the highest 30-day readmission rates. The first two weeks after discharge carry the greatest risk.
